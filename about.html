Why we are named Midas

In the old Greek story, King Midas turned everything he touched into gold. It sounds like a gift until he cannot eat or drink. The gold had no direction behind it.

We took the opposite lesson. Results only matter when someone planned them, so every Midas project starts with research and ends with a report. In between, one team handles the content, the ads and the creators.

We don't just market brands. We help move them forward.
